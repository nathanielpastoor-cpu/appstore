Privacy Policy — Today

Effective July 10, 2026

Today is a personal to-do app (com.nathaniel.todoapp) made by Nathaniel Pastoor. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and your choices. The short version: it stores your account email and your tasks so they sync across your devices, and nothing else. No ads, no analytics, no selling your data.

What the app collects

  • Email address — used to create and sign in to your account, via Google Firebase Authentication (email & password).
  • Your content — the tasks, lists, and related items you create, stored via Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) so they stay in sync across your devices.

The app does not collect location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics, and contains no third-party ads or trackers.

Why it collects it

Solely to make the app work — to sign you in and to store and sync the tasks you choose to save. That's it.

Who it's shared with

Your data is not sold or shared with third parties for their own use. It is stored and processed on your behalf by Google Firebase (Google Cloud Platform), which acts as the hosting and infrastructure provider. See Firebase's privacy and security information.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored within Google Firebase, protected by Google's infrastructure security. Access is restricted to your authenticated account.

Data retention & deletion

Your account and content are kept for as long as you use the app. To delete your account and all associated data, email nathaniel.pastoor@gmail.com and it will be removed.

Children

Today is not directed to children under 13, and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The effective date above will be revised when it does.

Contact

Questions? Email nathaniel.pastoor@gmail.com.
