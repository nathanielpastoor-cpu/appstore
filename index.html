Installing (first time only)

  1. Tap Download on any app above.
  2. Android will warn you the file "can harm your device." That warning appears for every app not from the Play Store. Tap Download anyway.
  3. Open the downloaded file. You'll be asked to allow installs from your browser — tap Settings, enable it, then go back.
  4. Tap Install.

Updates install over the old version and keep your data, as long as the version number went up. If an update refuses to install, uninstall the old one first and reinstall — that means the signing key changed.
